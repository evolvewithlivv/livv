# LIVV Brand Voice

Evolve With LIVV LLC · Internal product language

## What LIVV means

**L**ongevity · **I**ntegrity · **V**itality · **V**igilance

Core line: **Evolve with purpose. Built on LIVV.**

LIVV exists so people become more capable, disciplined, resilient, self-sufficient, intentional, and prepared for real life. Not to keep them dependent on an app.

The quote library (Aurelius, Epictetus, Seneca, and related sources) sets the texture: clarity, judgment, action. LIVV is stoic-adjacent in tone, not a Stoicism product.

## Voice traits

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Direct | Corporate |
| Calm and firm | Hype or hustle-bro |
| Concrete | Vague motivational fluff |
| Respectful of the user's time | Salesy or manipulative |
| Stoic-adjacent (clarity, action) | Soft self-help or therapy-speak |
| Premium by restraint | Loud luxury or crypto energy |

Voice test: *Would a serious adult respect this sentence on a quiet morning?*

## How we write

- Short sentences. One idea at a time.
- Prefer verbs and outcomes over adjectives.
- Name the thing: sleep, training, money, attention. Avoid jargon.
- No em dashes. Prefer periods, commas, or colons.
- Metrics support the story. They are not the opening line.
- Paid tiers describe **capability** (tools, protocols, depth), not cosmetics first.
- Empty states tell the truth. No fake encouragement.

### Prefer

- "Log what is true."
- "Build the session."
- "Hold the baseline."
- "How far the system goes."
- "No pieces live yet."

### Avoid

- "Unlock your potential"
- "Crush your goals"
- "Level up your life"
- "Your journey starts here"
- "We've got you"
- SaaS filler ("seamless", "delightful", "powerful suite")

## Room tone

Every screen is a **room**, not a dashboard.

| Room | Opening energy |
|------|----------------|
| **Home** | Orientation and perspective (quote-first) |
| **Daily** | One honest question, then action |
| **Train** | Energy with control — intent over utility |
| **Mind** | Editorial, serious ideas, continuous text |
| **Health** | Lifestyle baseline, not medical dashboard |
| **Progress** | Record of work done — evidence, not vanity |
| **Profile** | Identity and membership — "this is my LIVV" |
| **Membership** | How far the system goes with you |
| **Shop** | Brand and Collection, not a generic storefront |
| **Settings** | Control the system — calm utility |

## Visual kill list (copy-adjacent)

- Repetitive rounded cards on every section
- Dashboard syndrome (widgets before meaning)
- Too much text before the primary substance
- Generic card templates that feel identical across rooms
- Meaningless gamification language (XP inflation as the story)

## Membership language

- Spark is a **complete** product, not a crippled demo.
- Higher tiers unlock **Vault tools, protocols, programs** — real capability.
- Cosmetics (themes, marks) are secondary, never the headline.
- Never invent urgency or scarcity around membership.

## Collection / Shop

- Only list products when they are actually available.
- Embers are credit toward Collection, not cash and not giveaways.
- Honest empty states beat fake catalog energy.

## Test line

Before shipping copy, ask: **Would a serious adult respect this sentence on a quiet morning?**

If not, rewrite.
